Wheel of Luck for s&box
=======================

A port of Blue's Slots "Wheel of Luck" for Garry's Mod to an s&box Sandbox addon.
Original code, sounds, paytable and textures: Blue's Slots - Wheel Of Luck (GMod addon),
https://github.com/In-memory-of-CODE-BLUE/Blues-Slots-Wheel-Of-Luck, released under the
MIT License below. The original 3D model and its textures are marked "Model by Zerochain"; see "Model" below.
